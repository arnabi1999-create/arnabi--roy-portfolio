About

Data-led paid media, built around qualified leads.

I'm a performance-focused PPC specialist based in Kolkata. I started in paid media in 2022 as a PPC and SEO intern, moved into a full-time PPC Executive role in 2023, and since June 2025 I've been a PPC Specialist at Sourcedesk Global.

Strategy & structure
Combined high-intent Search campaigns with Performance Max within a full-funnel (TOFU–MOFU–BOFU) plan and monthly media plans matched to budgets and seasonal demand.

Challenge: keeping cost per lead low and lead quality high across five very different industries. Solution: tight intent-based keyword targeting, ongoing negative-keyword and audience exclusions, and a Search + Performance Max structure.
Results
  • Conversion rates improved 4–5% for service-based clients within the first month of the Performance Max + Search strategy.
  • Reduced cost per lead while improving lead quality and conversion rates.
  • Achieved 70–80% ROI through iterative creative, messaging and audience testing.
